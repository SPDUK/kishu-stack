# VELDRIFT concept showcase

Mode: Persuade through an atmospheric world. Audience: people evaluating the quality of Kishu’s design work. Primary action: explore the destinations, then read the local case study.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The horizon is only the start. An imagined MMO world invites curiosity through light, landscape, and the possibility of traveling together.

OWN-WORLD: Ink blue, ivory, and brass; GFS Didot display lettering and Geist interface copy; original landscape and human cartographer imagery; a fine geometric compass emblem.

STORY: Meet VELDRIFT, take a quiet editorial pause, choose Lantern Coast / Glassroot Vale / Astral Reach, meet the Wayfarers, and inspect how Kishu made the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Travelers and a copper-roofed coastal city at left, a large two-line invitation at right, and one clear exploration action. Narrow screens use a portrait source and move the copy beneath the subject.

FORM: Preserve the already approved cinematic composition while replacing setting, prose, imagery, and identity with original work. This scoped replacement does not reopen direction selection.

FINISH: Final art crops, responsive copy, keyboard behavior, reduced motion, links, and contrast must be checked against a fresh render. No final screenshot review is claimed by this surface record.
